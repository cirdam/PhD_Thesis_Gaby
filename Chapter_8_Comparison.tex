\chapter{Comparison with other experiment}
\label{chapter:comparison}

With the measurement developed from the \sd{433} data, the update of the \sd{750} spectrum and further the combination between the two measurements, the second knee could finally be measured.
The \sd{433} measurement of the second knee is constrained by the fewer statistics in the dataset, the width of the transition cannot be asserted.
On the other hand, the \sd{750} measurement has a much higher statistical weight but the full efficiency is reached $\lg(E/\mathrm{eV})=17.2$ and the position of the break nor the spectral index before the second knee cannot be measured.
Combining the strong points of the two measurements, the combined spectrum can be finally be developed and the second knee properly characterized in the fit.
There is good agreement between the two spectral indices before and after the second knee between the \sd{433} and the combined spectrum.
