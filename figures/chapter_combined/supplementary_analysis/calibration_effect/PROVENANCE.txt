Preview only: SD-750 energy and intrinsic flux changes from recalibration.

No thesis or main-repository files were changed.

Reproduce with:
MPLCONFIGDIR=/tmp/sd750-preview2-mpl /home/pbrichetto/sdana/.venv/bin/python prepare_calibration_effect.py

The two panels follow the Chapter5 calibration relation and the Chapter6
change-of-variables formula (eq:change_of_variables and
 eq:calibration_flux_transformation), allowing both A and B to change.
The exact nominal coefficients are retained rather than the rounded values
printed in Chapter5:
2021: A=13.2 PeV, B=1.002.
2025: A=13.2912 PeV, B=.9985676.

At fixed shower size, E2025=A2025*(E2021/A2021)^(B2025/B2021).
The left panel shows (E2025-E2021)/E2021 against log10(E2021/eV).

At fixed numerical energy E, define
Eold(E)=A2021*(E/A2025)^(B2021/B2025),
and use the full density Jacobian:
J2025(E)=J2021(Eold)*(B2021/B2025)*(Eold/E).
The right panel shows [J2025(E)-J2021(E)]/J2021(E).
It does NOT insert an energy-dependent shift into the constant-shift special
case. The spectrum is the intrinsic published2021 SD-750 model (Eq11,
central Table6 parameters), with no response folding. The turn near
log10(E/eV)=18.6 follows the ankle in that reference model.
No observed data points, sample updates, exposures or bad-period effects
enter either panel. No parameter uncertainties are propagated.

Analytical checks verify identity calibration, invertibility, and the
power-law scaling Jnew/Jold=r^(gamma-1) for a constant energy scale r.
All checks pass to approximately1e-15. The PDF was rendered with pdftoppm
and visually checked for layout, clipping and label overlap.

Suggested caption:
Effect of the updated SD-750 calibration on the assigned energy (left) and
the differential flux (right). The energy shift is evaluated at fixed shower
size, whereas the flux change compares both spectra at the same numerical
energy and includes the change-of-variables Jacobian. The published2021
spectral model is used as the reference. Nominal calibration coefficients
are used, without propagating parameter uncertainties.
