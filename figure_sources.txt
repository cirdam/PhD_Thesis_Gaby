directly by the chapter. Source components used to assemble the tank figure

sd_muon_calibration_bertou2006.png
Figure: fig:sd_muon_calibration in Chapter_3_Auger.tex.
Source: X. Bertou et al. (Pierre Auger Collaboration), Calibration of the surface
array of the Pierre Auger Observatory, Nuclear Instruments and Methods in
Physics Research A 568 (2006) 839-846, Figure 1.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nima.2006.07.066
Source PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.01656 (PDF page 3, downloaded 2026-10-29).
Bibliography key: Bertou:2006:Calibration.
Extraction: pdftoppm, page 3 scaled to 5400 pixels along its longest edge;
crop x=810, y=240, width=2190, height=1050 pixels.
Adaptation consists of extracting the two plots and replacing the original
caption with a thesis caption; plotted data, axes and legends are unchanged.
Both distributions sum the three PMTs; their charge conversion factor is about
1.09. The individual-PMT factor 1.03 +/- 0.02 is distinguished in the chapter text.
