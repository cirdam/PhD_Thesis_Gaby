\section{Effect of the updated energy calibration}

\Cref{fig:sd750_flux_difference_calibration} shows the relative energy shift introduce by the updated \sd{750} calibration and the corresponding analytical change in the flux.
The energy shift follows from the calibration relation in \cref{eq:sd750_cal}, evaluated at the same shower size for the two calibrations.
The flux transformation uses \cref{eq:change_of_variables,eq:calibration_flux_transformation}, including the Jacobian for the energy-dependent change of scale and the combined spectrum from EPJC 2021 as a reference.

\section{Comparison with the published spectral parameters}
\label{app:sd750_published_comparison}

For this comparison, the updated \sd{750} sample is fitted with the fixed shape parameters used in Table~6 of \cite{PierreAuger:2021:InfillSpectrum}: $\gamma_0=2.64$, $E_{01}=1.24\times10^{17}\,\mathrm{eV}$ and $\omega_{12}=0.05$.
The parameters $J_0$, $\omega_{01}$, $\gamma_1$, $E_{12}$ and $\gamma_2$ remain free.
The updated event selection, exposure and detector response are used, together with the fit interval $17.2<\lg(E/\mathrm{eV})<19.5$.
This check therefore shares the published fixed shape parameters, but does not reproduce the earlier dataset or all its fit conditions: the published spectrum starts at $\lg(E/\mathrm{eV})=17.0$ and uses an exposure of $105\pm4\,\mathrm{km^2\,sr\,yr}$.
The comparison refit gives $D/\mathrm{dof}=\SDSevenFiftyComparisonDeviance/\SDSevenFiftyComparisonDof$, with $p=\SDSevenFiftyComparisonPValue$.

\begin{figure}[htbp]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figures/chapter_combined/sd750_epjc2021_parameter_comparison.pdf}
    \caption[SD-750 spectral parameters compared with the 2021 result]{Spectral parameters from a refit of the updated \sd{750} sample using the published fixed shape parameters, compared with Table~6 of \cite{PierreAuger:2021:InfillSpectrum}.
    The updated data selection, exposure, response and fit interval are retained.
    Error bars show statistical uncertainties only.
    The normalization $J_0$ is expressed in units of $10^{-13}\,\mathrm{km^{-2}\,sr^{-1}\,yr^{-1}\,eV^{-1}}$; both models use the reference energy $10^{17}\,\mathrm{eV}$.}
    \label{fig:sd750_published_parameter_comparison}
\end{figure}

The fitted parameters are listed in \cref{tab:sd750_published_parameter_comparison}.
The largest visible changes occur in $J_0$ and $\omega_{01}$, whose one-standard-deviation statistical intervals do not overlap with the published intervals.
Their shifts are smaller than the corresponding published systematic uncertainties.
Because the samples overlap and the analysis conditions differ, this comparison is not an independent statistical test of agreement.
