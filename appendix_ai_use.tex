\chapter{Statement on AI Use}
\label{app:ai-use}

I declare that the scientific content of this thesis is my own work.
The research questions, methodology, data analysis, figures, interpretation of results, conclusions, and selection and verification of references were carried out by me.

This declaration was prepared taking into account the European Commission's \emph{Living guidelines on the responsible use of generative AI in research}, in particular their emphasis on transparency, human responsibility, and accountability when using generative AI tools in research
\cite{europeancommission2026genai}.

During the preparation of this thesis, I used generative artificial-intelligence tools, in particular ChatGPT by OpenAI \cite{openai2026chatgpt}, as editorial support tools.
Their use was limited to linguistic and presentational assistance, including grammar and spelling correction, improvement of text flow and readability, suggestions for alternative wording, translation or polishing of self-written passages, and identification of typographical or stylistic inconsistencies.

These tools were not used to generate original scientific results, to perform or replace the data analysis, to draw conclusions, to fabricate or modify data, or to provide unverified references.
All AI-assisted text was reviewed, edited, and approved by me.
I remain fully responsible for the accuracy, integrity, and proper citation of the content of this thesis.
